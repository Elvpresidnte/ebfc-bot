# EBFC Bot: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 27 September 2026_

EBFC Bot ("the app") is an unofficial fan app that posts Eastbourne Borough FC news and match information to r/EastbourneBoroFC.

**What the app collects about people: nothing.** The app does not read, collect, store, share or sell any personal information about Reddit users or anyone else. It doesn't look at users' profiles, votes, comments or messages.

**What the app does store.** Using Reddit's built-in app storage (Redis), the app keeps a short list of which news articles, results and fixtures it has already posted, plus when the league table was last posted. These entries are identifiers of public web pages on the club's website, not personal data. They exist only so the app doesn't post the same thing twice.

**Outside services.** The app reads public pages on the official club website, `ebfc.co.uk`. These requests are made by Reddit's servers on the app's behalf and carry no information about Reddit users. The club's website has its own privacy policy, which applies if you follow a link to it.

**Settings.** Subreddit moderators can change the app's settings (which post types are on, preview timing). Those settings are stored by Reddit as part of the app installation.

**Removal.** If moderators uninstall the app, it stops running. Its stored list of posted items is only used by this app and is not shared with anyone.

**Contact.** Questions can be sent via modmail to r/EastbourneBoroFC. Changes to this policy will be published in this file.
